(Updated) It is discovered that the vendored Node.js in the latest Electron
stable contains Node.js with Brotli RCE.  It is recommended to not use the
products until it can be remediated.

There has been a security flaw circulating in the socials and threat
intelligence community about Signal during the PHP RCE incident.  It deals with
improper closure of the microphone and camera after a hang up.  The AI
said that it also affects signal-desktop and allows a malicious threat actor
to continue to snoop and still stream audio to them after hitting hang up.

This advisory is mostly a warning aimed at FAFO users that have
a habit of putting off updating signal-desktop or updates in general.

Severity:  Critical, 9.3 score with CVSS 4

CVE-2026-113380 - ICUC, EBR, DT, ID

DT - Data Tampering
EBR - Extended Blast Radius
ID - Information Disclosure
ICUC - Improper Check for Unusual or Exceptional Conditions

Affected versions for CVE-2026-113380

<= 8.29.0

Affected versions for Brotli RCE.

Electron 45.0.0
Electron 44.7.2 vendors 

Remediation

(Update) It is recommended to stop using the product until the Electron is
updated with a more secure statically linked Node.js in Electron.  The
lockfile has to be updated again this time to Electron 46.0.0-alpha.1.  It
is not possible with stable release of Electron.  Either uninstall or use
MAC (e.g. AppArmor or SELinux) for the /opt/Signal executables and
`kill -9 $(pgrep signal-desktop)`.

The latest Electron is ineffective for the Brolti RCE mitigation based on
version audit.

It is recommended to wait for the oiledmachine-overlay ebuild to upgrade the
Electron 46 update corresponding to Chromium 158.0.8114.0, Node 24.22.1.

The distro's version uses Electron 44.7.2 corresponding to the insecure Chromium
152.0.7977.149.  In addition, it uses the vulnerable vendored Brotli in the
vendored Node.js.
