As I said earlier, the use of virtual machine is not popular in my social
circle.  It is a low priority to fix.  However, some of you may find it
important and relevant.

The vulnerability may allow the guest virtual machine to steal sensitive
data from the host bare metal machine on AMD machines with nested paging
enabled.

Informal description:  KVM hypervisor escape
Vulnerability ID:  CVE-2026-99312
Impact vectors:  HVE, DoS, DT, ID

HVE - HyperVisor Escape
DoS - Denial of Service
DT - Data Tampering
ID - Information Disclosure

Update to the following kernels:
>= 7.3.5 on the 7.3 series
>= 6.18.64 on the 6.18 series
>= 6.12.118 on the 6.12 series

Any kernel flavor will fix this issue.

This news item uses AI inference for details.

There were reported vulnerabilites that you may missed
covered by independent media:

"NoisyTCP, CVE-2026-99288;RC, IV, DoS;11-year-old bug that crashes servers"
"Fragnesia, CVE-2026-46300;AEITW, KEV, ACP, PCC, LPE, UVAR, DoS, DT, ID;7.8 High on CVSS 3, ACP in PHP RCE"
"PwnEscape, CVE-2026-99999;EBR, VME, CE, IV, DoS, DT, ID;9.3 Critical on CVSS 3"
"CVE-2026-98432;ZC, RC, UAF, RCE, DoS, DT, ID;9.8 Critical on CVSS 3, MPTCP RCE"
"CVE-2026-99312;HVE, DoS, DT, ID;9.8 high, KVM hypervisor escape"

While this bug receives media attention there are more than a dozen
vulnerabilities under reported by the media.  There are vulnerabilities
like UAF an TOCTOU fixed in the recent point releases that may be more
relevant to you instead.
