It is discovered that Electron 45 and earlier used a vendored copy
of Node.js containing a vendored copy of Brotli with RCE.  It is recommened
at this time to either uninstall all Electron apps or add a global deny
rule for MAC (e.g. AppArmor or SELinux) for binaries/opt/<PKGNAME> files
not the /usr/bin/<program-name> shell wrapper.

`equery f <pkgname>` can help find locate the folder to temporary blacklist
until the ebuild lockfiles are brought up to date with Electron 46.

Affected packages:

All Electron apps
app-misc/llocal
media-gfx/upscayl
net-im/signal-desktop
media-gfx/blockbench
media-gfx/upscayl
media-video/lossless-cut
www-apps/LobeHub
