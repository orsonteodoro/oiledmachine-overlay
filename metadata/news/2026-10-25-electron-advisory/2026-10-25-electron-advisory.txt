(Updated Oct 28, 2026) The lockfiles will be updated to use Electron 45.1.0
to fix this issue.

(Updated Oct 26, 2026) It is discovered that Electron 45.0.0 and earlier used a
vendored copy of Node.js containing a vendored copy of Brotli with RCE.  If an
app is capable of automatically loading or passively click loading a jxl image
using metadata compressed with Brotli, the app is at risk.  It is recommened at
this time to either uninstall all Electron apps or add a global deny rule for
MAC (e.g. AppArmor or SELinux) for executables in /opt/<PKGNAME> not the
/usr/bin/<program-name> shell wrapper and to kill all running Electron based
apps.

`equery f <pkgname>` can help find locate the folder to temporary blacklist.

Affected packages:

All Electron apps
app-misc/llocal
www-apps/LobeHub
Electron 45.0.0 - with Node 24.22.0 [old version used in oiledmachine-overlay]
Electron 44.9.1 - with Node 24.22.0 [used in upstream's signal-desktop 8.32.0 release]
Electron 44.7.2 - with Node 24.21.0 [used in the distro's signal-desktop-bin 8.31.0 ebuild]
