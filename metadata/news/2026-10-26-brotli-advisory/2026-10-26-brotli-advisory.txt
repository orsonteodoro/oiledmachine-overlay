It is discovered that multiple packages have vendored Brotli.  If an
app is capable of automatically loading, passively click loading, or loading or
clicking a URL to a JPEG XL (jxl) image using metadata compressed with Brotli,
or can load custom woff2 fonts, then the app is at risk.  It is recommened at
this time to either uninstall all affected apps or add a global deny rule for
MAC (e.g. AppArmor or SELinux) for executables and to kill all running apps
that used an old vendored copy of Brotli.  The init daemon services need to be
disabled temporarily until the lockfiles are updated.
