cURL had a non-transparent silent update about an ACE/RCE fix but only discovered
later through in social media post about PHP RCE.  The repo has updated the
fallback-commit to the latest to address it and other newer security issues.  On
the social media they say mostly use-after-free but rarely RCE.  The AI says it
is both ACE and RCE.

CVE-2026-92392 - 8.1 High - ACE/RCE, UAF, DoS, DT, ID
SSVC - POC

ACE - Arbitrary Code Execution
DoS - Denial of Service
DT - Data Tampering
ID - Information Disclosure
UAF - Use After Free

CVE-<YEAR>-<NUMBER> - A vulnerability ID
POC - Proof of Concept
SSVC - Stakeholder Specific Vulnerability Categorization [how fast to patch]

The AI said that the POC is used for a decision tree to determine urgency based
on exposure of the type of asset.  Left of > is local context.  Right of > is
the decision outcome produced by the decision tree.  Each decision tree outcome
gives you how fast to remediate.

 POC shifts to Active [explotation] > Act    -- Remediate 48 hours
             Mission critical asset > Attend -- Remediate in 14 days
     Not universally critical asset > Track* -- Remediate in 60 days
                 Non-critical asset > Track  -- Remediate in 90 days

Current mitigations

Option 1:  Simply emerge cURL.

emerge -1vO "=net-libs/curl-9999::oiledmachine-overlay"

Option 2:  Alternatively, with the new secure-version.eclass, you can switch to
stable.  To switch to stable, do this...

Add to /etc/portage/make.conf:

CURL_PV="8.23.0"

Add to /etc/portage/package.mask:

net-libs/curl::oiledmachine-overlay

Then finally:

emerge -1vO "=net-libs/curl-0.23.0::gentoo"
