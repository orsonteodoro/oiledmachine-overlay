Due to various quirks in the software ecosystem, the eMudhra intermediate
Certificate Authority (CA) removal may not be consistently applied.  The overlay
has used hard masks to ensure consistent security.  The industry as a whole has
decided to reject or remove eMudhra intermediate CA from the cert database due
to insecure private keys, obtained by brute forcing.  In social media, they gave
the example "4" as the private key.  The AI said that the private keys were
under 256 and can be discovered in less than a second.

The benefit of rejecting this intermediate CA is to mitigate gullible users from
phishing attacks or MITM (Man In The Middle) attacks that lead to stolen crown
jewels, stolen keys to the crown jewels, or harm.

This advisory was initially targeted for vendored bundled certificates packages.
It also applies users who have just returned to to their machine but haven't
updated or are putting off updates.

Affected packages

app-misc/ca-certificates with USE=certdata-esr
dev-games/godot-editor with USE=portable
dev-games/godot-export-templates-bin with embedded fallback certificate bundle

Recommended update by pypi

The first 3 were requested updated by PyPi to mitigate a MITM attack.  The
others were found in the "See more" link
(https://discuss.python.org/t/information-for-python-users-regarding-emudhra-emsign-ca-certificate-security-incident/109576)

An additional post said that the pip will combine the [compromised] vendored
certificate bundle with the system's certificate bundle.

Reasons per package

The version for NSS (ESR) hasn't been bumped preventing certdata.txt update on
eMudhra intermediate CA removal.  The browser gets first class treatment but the
NSS ESR is a third class citizen.  It is like last place in certs update.  Users
will be forced temporarily into picking one of certdata-live, certdata-live-af,
certdata-latest (ebuild default).  The USE flag temporary ban for certdata-esr
will be lifted when the ESR is version bumped with a certdata.txt update.

The godot-editor if using portable will use a vendored copy of bundled
certificates if the system certificate bundle is accidently removed.

Projects godot-export-templates-bin may likely embed vendored copy of older
certificates.  The other reason for the package mask is because it is affected
by Brotli2Shell.

Godot games or Godot app projects (e.g. chat or messengers) can override
certificate bundle by setting the settings in the link
(https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/networking/ssl_certificates.html).
The engine looks at the override certificate bundle first, then system
certificate bundle store, then the fallback vendored certificate bundle.  The
scenario where the certificate override applies is where a user runs the portable
game/app in an older computer with older operating system long term support (LTS)
release version.
