This overlay already mitigates the vulnerability.  The oiledmachine-overlay uses
the live ebuild as the default.  It treats the PDF stack as high priority
monitored asset to mitigate zero-click attacks like the 2021 FORCEDENTRY attack.
The AI said that the advisory was released on Nov 21, 2026.  The ebuild had
triaged earlier at 14b98b1 (Oct 2026).  It is recommended to update to the
latest commit (Nov 20, 2026 or newer at 41bdda6) to mitigate newer
vulnerabilities/bugs.

Triage history of the ghostscript-gpl ebuild:

Nov 22, 2026 - BOR, OF, UB, shift overflow mitigation
Oct 28, 2026 - Brotli RCE mitigation
Oct 11, 2026 - OF, UF, HO, BOR mitigation
Sep 11, 2026 - Upstream hardening
Sep 7, 2027 - Upstream remediation
Sep 3, 2026 - IL mitigation

This security advisory is primarily for users that have a Wordpress website
who accept uploads or who have not updated their systems for months after
reading this advisory.

Aliases:  Upload2Shell, -dSAFER bypass vulnerability
Vulnerability ID:  CVE-2026-125396
Impact vectors:  SBE, IV, UAF, VS(DoS, DT, ID) SS(None)
Severity:  High, 8.5 score CVSS 4, 7.8 score CVSS 3

SBE - SandBox Escape
IV - Insufficient Validation
UAF - Use After Free
VS - Vulnerable direct system

Nov 2026 vulnerabilities/bugs:
Buffer overrread (BOR)
Overflow (OF)
Shift overflow (UB)
Undefined behavior (UB)
