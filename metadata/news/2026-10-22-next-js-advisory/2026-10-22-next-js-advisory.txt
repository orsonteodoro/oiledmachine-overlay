The Next.js has a RCE vulnerability that can be exploited during the runtime use
of an application.  In the upcoming days, it will be fixed.  Due to a emerge
update backlog and lack of staffing it will be delayed.  A lockfile update is
needed.

CVE-2025-55182 - RCE, ISD, EBR, ZC, DoS, DT, ID, critical severity at 10 score with CVSS 3.0
CVE-2025-66478 - Rejected as duplicate of CVE-2025-55182
CVE-2026-96889 - ACE/RCE, UAF, DoS, DT, ID, high severity at 7 score with CVSS 3.0
GHSA-673q-mjm6-r5xr - EBR, ZC, DoS, DT, ID, critical severity at 9.5 with CVSS 4.0

ACE - Arbitrary Code Execution
DoS - Denial of Service
DT - Data Tampering
EBR - Extended Blast Radius
ID - Information Disclosure
ISD - Insecure Deseralization
RCE - Remote Code Execution
UAF - Use After Free
ZC - Zero Click Vulnerability (AV:N, PR:N, UI:N)

Patched versions of Next.js

>= 16.4.1
>= 15.5.28 (EOL)

Affected status of potential consumers of Node.js according to AI:

app-misc/amica - No low risk based on configuration
media-gfx/upscayl - No risk
www-apps/LobeHub - High risk
www-misc/vercel monorepo and the vercel cli utility - No/indirectly

Current mitigations

Do not use the affected apps until lockfiles have been updated, uninstall
the program, remove the executable bit with chmod, or add a global deny
rule with your MAC (e.g. AppArmor or SELinux) for the binaries in the
/opt/<PROGRAM-NAME> folder and not the shell wrapper in
/usr/bin/<PROGRAM-NAME>.

Remeditiation

Use the current recommended mitigation at this time.  This section will
be updated when lockfile is ready.
